5. **Kill then remove may race** (`programs.rs:39`, `places.rs:56-57`).

   - Fix: skip `keep` and `kill` when `state == "exited"`. Call `hc wait` or poll `get` until the terminal has exited before `rm`. Treat a `not_found` from `rm` as success.
